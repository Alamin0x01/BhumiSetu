02 · Logo icon

Anatomy of the mark

Flat, geometric and built on a 4-unit gap grid. It works without text and holds up from favicon size to signage.

  1. Parcel banks

    Land plots on both sides of the span. The gaps between them are the grid lines.

  2. Deck and bowstring arch

    The bridge itself: one emerald structure that connects the two banks.

  3. Record nodes

    Linked documents sitting on the arch, tied to the deck by hangers.

  4. Evidence node

    The single amber accent at the crown. It marks the point of verification.

  5. Ring cut-outs

    True negative space around each node, so the mark stays crisp on any background.

05 · Color

Palette

Navy carries trust and structure, emerald carries connection and validation, amber is reserved for the one thing that needs a reviewer.

Deep Navy#0F172A · RGB 15 23 42
Land plots, wordmark, app icon tile, text.
Emerald Green#10B981 · RGB 16 185 129
Bridge, “Setu”, validation states.
Amber Accent#F59E0B · RGB 245 158 11
Evidence node only. One per mark.

Suggested balance: about 60% navy, 30% emerald, 10% amber. White is the supporting neutral.

06 · Typography

Type

Wordmark

Manrope ExtraBold (800), outlined. Do not retype it, always use the supplied files.

Tagline

Manrope SemiBold (600), uppercase, +0.12 em tracking, 68% navy on light backgrounds. “Evidence-First Land Record Review”.

Manrope is licensed under the SIL Open Font License. For product UI, pair the logo with Manrope or a neutral sans such as Inter or Segoe UI.

07 · Usage

Clear space and minimum size

Clear space

Keep at least twice the diameter of the amber node free on every side.

Minimum size

Icon: 16 px wide. Horizontal lockup with tagline: 240 px wide. Below that, use the icon alone.

Please don’t

✕ Recolor
✕ Stretch or squash
✕ Add shadows or effects
✕ Use on low-contrast color
